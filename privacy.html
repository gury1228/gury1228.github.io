YouTube-Drive-Backup

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

YouTube-Drive-Backup is a personal, non-commercial, self-hosted workflow operated by its owner. It uses rclone and the Google Drive API to upload files to Google Drive accounts the owner has authorized.

Information accessed

When the owner authorizes access, the workflow may access Google Drive file names, identifiers, metadata, and contents to the extent granted by the drive.file OAuth permission. Its intended purpose is to create folders, upload files, list its uploads, and verify uploaded content.

Use of information

Google Drive data is used only to operate the owner's upload and verification workflow. It is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics, or sale. The workflow does not send Drive data or OAuth tokens to this informational website.

Storage and sharing

Uploaded files are kept in the owner's Google Drive accounts. OAuth credentials are stored in the owner's self-hosted rclone configuration and are not published through this website. The workflow is not a multi-user hosted service and does not intentionally share personal Drive data with third parties.

Retention and deletion

The owner controls the stored Drive files and may remove them in Google Drive. The owner may revoke OAuth access from Google Account security settings and remove the local rclone configuration to disconnect the workflow. Temporary source files may be deleted by the operator's workflow only after upload verification is successful.

Website hosting

This static information site contains no sign-in forms, cookies set by application code, or application analytics. The hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process access logs and technical information under GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact

Questions about this personal project may be submitted through GitHub Issues. Do not post OAuth tokens or private file details in public issues.
