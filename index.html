YouTube-Drive-Backup
Personal · Self-hosted · Non-commercial

A personal workflow for saving files to Google Drive

YouTube-Drive-Backup is a personal, self-hosted workflow that helps its operator transfer locally prepared video and subtitle files to their own Google Drive storage using rclone and the Google Drive API.

What it does

  • Connects Google Drive accounts authorized by the account owner.
  • Uploads files and organizes them into folders in the owner's Drive.
  • Checks uploaded files before the operator's local workflow removes temporary copies.

This is a private utility, not a publicly available upload service. This website does not accept files, Google passwords, or OAuth authorization tokens.

Google Drive access

The workflow requests drive.file access for files it creates or that the user explicitly makes available to the app. OAuth credentials are held in the operator's local rclone configuration, not on this website. Files remain in Google Drive accounts controlled by the owner.

For details about data handling, please read the Privacy Policy.
